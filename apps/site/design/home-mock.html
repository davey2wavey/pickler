Where the money goes, and how long it lasts

Small teams leak cash in dozens of small bills and can't say at a glance how many months are left. Runway is one ledger, one burn line, one number.

What it does

Every outgoing dollar in one table. Transfers excluded from spend. A forward line shows the lowest cash point in the next 180 days.

Under the hood

Postgres with row-level security, server actions, magic-link auth, Playwright on every PR.
